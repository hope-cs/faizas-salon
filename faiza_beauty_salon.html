Our Story

More than beauty —
it's confidence

Faiza's Beauty Salon was founded in 2000 with a simple belief: every woman deserves to feel beautiful, cared for, and confident. Starting from a single location in Islamabad, Faiza built her salon around one thing — genuine relationships with every client who walks through the door.

Today, with two branches in F-6 and F-7 Markaz, our team of skilled beauty professionals continues that same tradition. We don't rush. We listen. We work with your features, your style, and your occasion to bring out the best version of you.

"Every woman who sits in our chair leaves more confident than when she arrived — that is our promise."

Whether you're preparing for your wedding, a family event, or simply want a relaxing afternoon of self-care, Faiza's is your home away from home in Islamabad.
